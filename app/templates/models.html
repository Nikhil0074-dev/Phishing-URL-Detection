What each model brings

Logistic Regression

Linear baseline. Fast, interpretable coefficients, a useful lower bound for the others.

Naive Bayes

Probabilistic baseline that assumes feature independence; usually the fastest to train.

Decision Tree

A single tree of interpretable if/else rules over the URL features.

Random Forest

An ensemble of decision trees; typically more accurate and less prone to overfitting than one tree.

SVM

Finds a maximum-margin boundary in the scaled feature space; strong on high-dimensional data.

XGBoost

Gradient boosted trees added one at a time to correct earlier errors; often the top performer here.

ANN

A small multi-layer perceptron; can capture non-linear feature interactions the linear models miss.
